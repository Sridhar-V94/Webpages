How the simulator treats cash flows

Buyer pays down payment, stamp/registration and other purchase costs at the start; EMI, maintenance and property tax during ownership. Renter invests the buyer's initial cash outlay and invests the monthly amount by which buyer costs exceed rent. If renting costs more in a month, the model withdraws from the portfolio. Investment gains are taxed on liquidation at the selected effective rate. Rental deposit is treated as refundable capital tied up, not an expense.

Critical financial insights

Important: This is a scenario model, not personalised financial, tax or legal advice. Returns, property appreciation and rent escalation are uncertain. Actual home-loan tax deductions depend on tax regime, ownership, occupancy, loan purpose and prevailing law. Do not assume a tax benefit unless verified for your circumstances.
Model limitations: simplified monthly amortisation and monthly investment contributions; annual rent/maintenance escalation; no leverage refinancing, vacancy, major repairs, furnishing, brokerage on rent, capital-gains exemptions, reinvestment tax drag, asset-allocation volatility, or opportunity cost of future salary changes unless entered in assumptions. Investment gain tax is an effective-rate approximation, not a tax calculation.
